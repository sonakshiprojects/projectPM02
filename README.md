# Business Intelligence & Operational Analytics Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

An end-to-end **Business Intelligence and Operational Analytics solution** developed using **Power BI and MS Office** to analyze **73K+ operational records**, monitor KPIs, identify performance trends, and support data-driven decision-making.

The project focuses on converting operational data into actionable insights through **KPI reporting, trend analysis, dashboard-based drill-down, and Root Cause Analysis (RCA).**

## 🎯 Objectives

* Monitor key operational and business KPIs.
* Identify performance trends and deviations.
* Drill down into underperforming areas to investigate operational bottlenecks.
* Apply a structured **Root Cause Analysis (RCA)** approach.
* Present insights through interactive dashboards and executive reports.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI** – Dashboard development, KPI monitoring, interactive analysis
* **MS Excel** – Data organization, analysis, and reporting
* **MS PowerPoint** – Executive reporting and presentation of insights
* **AI-Assisted Analytics** – Analytical brainstorming and report refinement

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

1. **Data Understanding** – Reviewed the dataset structure and identified relevant business metrics.
2. **Data Preparation** – Checked and prepared the data for analysis and reporting.
3. **KPI Analysis** – Developed and monitored relevant performance indicators.
4. **Trend Analysis** – Examined performance patterns across relevant dimensions.
5. **RCA** – Investigated significant KPI deviations through segmentation and drill-down analysis to identify contributing factors.
6. **Reporting** – Presented key findings, observations, and recommendations through Power BI and executive reports.

## 📊 Key Outcomes

* Analyzed **73K+ operational records**.
* Improved visibility into KPI performance and operational trends.
* Identified areas requiring further operational investigation.
* Converted analytical findings into concise, decision-oriented reports.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides a centralized view of operational performance, enabling managers to **identify deviations, investigate potential bottlenecks, prioritize improvement areas, and make data-driven decisions.**
